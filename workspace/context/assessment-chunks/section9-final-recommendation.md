## 9. Final Recommendation

Build exactly this next, in this order:
- Month 1-2: Risk Correlation Engine (the core differentiator) — data model, ingestion, correlation logic, single-pane UI, validation with real CIO/CISO.
- Month 3: CIO + CLO Dashboards (consume the correlation engine).
- Month 4: Separate Internal Audit from CRO; enforce auth (JWT, org isolation, CORS); evidence collection proof-of-concept.
- Month 5: Exception workflow; control drift detection; audit evidence repository.
- Month 6: Split App.jsx (no functionality loss); Board reports; documentation; SOC2 readiness.

Critical Success Question: Can you show a healthcare CIO a single finding screen and have them say "Yes, I understand exactly what this means, who owns it, and what to do next" in < 30 seconds? If yes, you have a product. If no, keep building the correlation engine.
